# DAYMARK — REPLIT DESIGN MASTER PROMPT

Design a visually exceptional, mobile-first memory app called **Daymark**.

## 1. PRODUCT IDEA

Daymark turns everyday memories into little gifts.

Users save meaningful moments with photos, videos, voice notes, written stories, dates, locations, moods, and the people who were there.

Instead of presenting memories as boring files, folders, or social-media posts, Daymark represents each saved memory as a beautifully wrapped digital gift.

The emotional idea is:

**Life gives you moments. Daymark helps you keep them.**

Main tagline:

**Every day leaves you a little something.**

Alternative:

**Keep the little gifts life gives you.**

The product should feel like something users WANT to open every day.

It should feel:

* joyful
* warm
* youthful
* emotional
* vibrant
* friendly
* highly polished
* character-driven
* nostalgic without looking vintage
* playful without looking childish

---

# 2. UI INSPIRATION

Use the following products only as UX and interaction references.

DO NOT copy their branding, screens, illustrations, characters, layouts, logos, or proprietary visual assets.

## Retro

Reference:
https://retro.app/

Study Retro for:

* memory-first product thinking
* photo-focused layouts
* intimate social experience
* emphasis on real friends rather than public followers
* clean photo presentation
* low-clutter interfaces
* making ordinary life feel meaningful

Daymark should borrow Retro's emotional simplicity while developing a completely original visual identity.

---

## Locket

Reference:
https://locket.camera/

Study Locket for:

* intimacy between close friends
* putting people before metrics
* simple photo interactions
* emotional immediacy
* making small everyday moments feel important

Daymark should have the same feeling of closeness without copying Locket's UI.

---

## Finch

Reference:
https://finchcare.com/

Study Finch primarily for:

* character integration
* mascot-led experiences
* friendly onboarding
* positive reinforcement
* character reactions
* progress celebrations
* playful empty states
* making a utility product emotionally engaging

DO NOT make Daymark look like Finch.

Daymark has its own mascot universe based around gifts and memories.

---

## How We Feel

Reference:
https://howwefeel.org/

Study How We Feel for:

* emotional color systems
* mood selection
* large touch-friendly controls
* approachable interfaces
* visual categorization
* making abstract emotions understandable through design

Use this inspiration specifically when designing Daymark's mood and emotion experience.

---

# 3. ORIGINAL DAYMARK VISUAL UNIVERSE

The references above should influence UX principles only.

Daymark must have its own recognizable universe.

The visual metaphor is:

MEMORY = GIFT

The recurring visual language should therefore include:

* gift boxes
* ribbons
* bows
* gift tags
* stickers
* photographs
* postcards
* stars
* hearts
* calendars
* handwritten notes
* flowers
* location pins
* little sparkles
* collectible objects

Avoid excessive galaxy imagery.

Stars can appear as supporting decorative elements, but the primary identity should be GIFT + MEMORY.

# 5. MAIN CHARACTER — MARKY

Create an original Daymark mascot called:

**Marky**

Marky is the Memory Keeper.

Marky is a small purple gift-box creature.

Characteristics:

* purple rounded gift-box body
* golden ribbon
* tiny heart charm
* expressive eyes
* small arms
* small legs
* expressive eyebrows
* subtle blush
* soft 3D appearance

Marky should feel adorable and expressive without looking like a children's cartoon.

Generate poses for:

* waving
* celebrating
* thinking
* sleeping
* surprised
* carrying photos
* holding a heart
* wrapping a memory
* opening a gift
* looking nostalgic
* cheering
* taking a photograph

Marky should appear naturally throughout the UI rather than being placed everywhere.

# 21. MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Add subtle animation throughout.

Examples:

Marky waves when opening Home.

Stella sparkles beside important memories.

Gift boxes gently bounce when hovered.

Opening memories unties ribbons.

Saving produces a small confetti burst.

Characters react to user actions.

Photos gently lift when hovered.

Buttons compress slightly when tapped.

Use animation intentionally.

Do NOT make everything move.

# 23. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Use approximately:

50% original Daymark identity

20% emotional memory-journal simplicity inspired by Retro

15% character integration principles inspired by Finch

10% intimacy principles inspired by Locket

5% emotional color categorization inspired by How We Feel

Do NOT visually reproduce any of those products.

Daymark should be recognizable without seeing its name.
